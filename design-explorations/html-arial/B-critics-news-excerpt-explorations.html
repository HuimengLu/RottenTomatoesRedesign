ARIAL · B · CRITICS REVIEWS
News excerpt explorations
Four ways to make critic reviews read like press coverage while staying in the Arial · B system. All keep the same section header, tabs and data. The only additions are the five news tokens below.
Kicker
OBSERVER (UK)
·
TOP CRITIC
Label 12/14 Bold caps +1.6px. Outlet in text colour, Top Critic in Coral on Tint / Action Red on light.
Coral · Brand Red on dark
TOP
New text colour #FF9372 for red text on dark surfaces (5.7:1 on Tint surface). Brand Red text there is only 3.3:1.
Rules
Heavy 2px (Projector White 85% on dark) opens a section; hairline 1px (14%) separates stories. Rules replace card boxes.
Byline
By Mark Kermode
· Jan 16, 2017
Small 14/20. Critic name in Bold, date Regular at muted. Always after the quote: content first.
Newsprint
Projector White surface with Black text, radius 4. Only for clippings (03).
NEWS EXCERPT · 01
Pull quote
Cards become ruled newspaper columns. The strongest top-critic line is set as a pull quote in the system’s Quote style, the rest run as column excerpts. Rules, not boxes, separate the voices.
KEEPS
Tabs, Top Critic, Tomatometer icon + score, Full review, all six reviews.
SYSTEM TOKENS
Quote 36/44 Bold · Body L 19/28 · Label 12/14 caps · Brand Red quote mark · Rule heavy 2px / hairline 1px.
TRADE-OFF
Lead pick needs an editorial rule (e.g. highest-voted top critic). Columns end at uneven heights.

NEWS EXCERPT · 03
Clippings
Reviews pinned up as press clippings: outlet as masthead over a double rule, a dated dateline, the quote in black on Projector White. Reads instantly as “what the press said”.
KEEPS
Tabs, Top Critic (Action Red on light), Tomatometer icon + score, Full review, all six reviews.
SYSTEM TOKENS
Projector White surface · Black text 18/27 · Masthead 24/26 Bold · Label 10/12 caps dateline · Radius 4.
TRADE-OFF
Light paper on the tint is the loudest option; keep ads off light surfaces nearby so clippings aren’t mistaken for sponsored units.

Wire
A dated news wire. Reviews run in publication order on a timeline, so the page tells how reception unfolded, from the first raves in January to the dissent in September.
